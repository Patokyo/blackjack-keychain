Blackjack keychain - by Patrick Scholefield

PAGE 1
Cover.
Blackjack keychain.
Test your blackjack skills with this compact keychain device.
Hone your basic strategy knowledge in train mode, then compete for a
highscore in play mode.

PAGE 2
Rules of Blackjack.
Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.
Each round starts with the player receiving two cards and the dealer
receiving one. These totals are shown on the display.
Pictures count ten, an ace one or eleven.
Two cards making 21 is blackjack, and beats any other 21.
The dealer stands on 17, and only draws after the player stands.

PAGE 3
Your move: the five buttons.
Hit: deal the player another card.
Stand: stay at the total you have.
Double: double the bet, and take only one extra card before auto
standing.
Split: create two separate hands out of your pair, doubling the bet, each
hand resolving in turn, up to four hands.
Bank: show the bankroll, and deal at the bet prompt.
Press one button at a time.

PAGE 4
Playing the hand out.
The player acts, then the dealer draws to 17, then the closest to 21
wins. The bank pays 1:1, a natural pays 3:2, and equal totals push.

PAGE 5
The 4 digit display and its lights.
The display is only four digits: the player's total on the left pair, the
dealer's on the right, with LEDs to say what the digits cannot.
Pair: the two cards are each half of the total, eg 18 with pair means two
9s.
Soft: an ace is counting as 11, and the LED goes out once the ace must be
1.
Win, push, lose: one of the three lights after every hand.
Right and wrong: train mode only, one of them marking each decision.
Letters stand in for words: BET, PLAY, TRN, HIGH.

PAGE 6
Train mode.
Two extra LEDs, wrong and right, grade every decision against basic
strategy. The game records the percentage of correct decisions in each
session, showing it after each hand and when the bank button is held.

PAGE 7
Play mode.
A bankroll to bet from, a bet to set before each hand, and a high score
to chase. The bank is kept between sessions. A short bank goes on the
next hand whole; only an empty bank ends the run.

PAGE 8
Basic strategy, the back page.
The reference sheet, as a grid: your hand down the side, the dealer's
up-card across the top, hit, stand, double or split in every cell. Hard
totals, then soft, then pairs by total.
