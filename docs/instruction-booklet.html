PLAY MODE

Switch the lever to PLAY. You start with $100, and the bank is kept, so a run carries on next time you switch it on.

Before each hand the display shows BET, then the amount. Set it with the action buttons: HIT adds $5, STAND takes $5 off, DOUBLE doubles it, SPLIT halves it.

Tap BANK to deal. The bet runs from $5 to $100 and never above what the bank can lose.

Hold BANK for the bankroll; keep holding to flash HIGH over your best bank.

A short bank goes on the next hand whole. An empty bank lights LOSE and ends the run: press any button for a fresh $100.

Reach 9999 and every light starts flashing. That is the jackpot.

05

THE HAND

1

You act. Hit, stand, double or split until your turn is over.

2

The dealer draws. It turns its hole card over and takes cards until it reaches 17.

3

Closest to 21 wins. Equal totals push and the bet comes back.

YOUR TOTALDEALER TOTAL

The dealer shows a 7 and draws a 10, standing on 17. Your 18 is closer, so the bank pays 1:1: a $10 bet pays $10.

A natural pays 3:2. Over 21 is a bust and loses at once.

WHAT TO PRINT

These two A4 pages are one sheet of paper, printed double-sided and flipping on the long edge, at 100% with no scaling. Side 1 carries the outside of the booklet and these notes; side 2 carries the inside.

WHAT IS ON THE SHEET

Three pieces, 100 × 70 mm. Each piece is one sheet of the booklet: fold it in half on its ticks and it is 50 × 70 mm, the size of the board. The tag beside each piece names its letter and the two pages on that face.

CHECK IT BEFORE YOU CUT

Hold the printed sheet up to a light. Page 2 must sit directly behind page 1, the right way up, and page 11 behind page 10. If the back of a piece shows a different page, or a page is upside down, the printer flipped the second side on the short edge: print again with long-edge, or book, binding.

ASSEMBLY

  1. Cut along the grey lines. A steel rule and a knife give a straighter edge than scissors. Nothing outside the lines is kept.
  2. Fold each piece in half along its ticks with the outside faces showing: on the piece with page 1, page 1 stays out. Running a blunt rule along the fold first makes it crisper.
  3. Nest the three pieces: the one with page 1 outermost, then the one with page 3 on its outer face, then the one with page 5.
  4. Staple twice on the fold, about 10 mm in from each end, through all three layers. Open the booklet and flatten the staple legs with a fingernail or the flat of the rule.

Page numbers sit at the foot of each page, odd on the right. A booklet that reads 1 to 12 in order, with no page upside down, is bound correctly. Nothing is printed outside these three pieces, so nothing is wasted.

THE RULES

Get closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over.

You are dealt two cards. The dealer gets one, face up.

YOUR TOTALDEALER UP-CARD

Pictures count ten. An ace counts 1 or 11, whichever helps.

Two cards making 21 is BLACKJACK, and it beats any other 21.

The dealer stands on 17.

THE FIRST HAND

1

Plug in. Any USB-C charger, either sort of cable. There is no switch for power; the board wakes when it is fed.

2

Pick a mode. The lever on the right edge: down for TRAIN, up for PLAY. The display shows the mode as the lever lands.

THE DISPLAY

Blackjack is usually played with cards, but the display is only four digits. Your total takes the left pair, the dealer's the right.

Two lights say what those digits are made of.

PAIR

The two cards match, so the hand may be split. A total of 18 with PAIR lit is two 9s.

SOFT

An ace is counting as 11. The light goes out the moment the ace has to count as 1.

A natural 21 shows but is never asked to act.

Letters stand in for words: BET, PLAY, TRN, HIGH.
