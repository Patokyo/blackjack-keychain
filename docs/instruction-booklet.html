Play mode

Play starts you with $100, and the bankroll is kept, so a run carries on next time you switch it on.

Before each hand the display shows BET, then the amount. HIT adds $5, STAND takes $5 off, DOUBLE doubles it, SPLIT halves it. Tap BANK to deal.

The bet runs from $5 to $100, and never above what the bank can lose.

Hold BANK at any time to read the bankroll.

Blackjack keychain · booklet

Print, fold, staple

These two A4 pages are one booklet. Print them double-sided on a single sheet, flipping on the long edge, at 100% with no scaling. Side 1 carries the outside of the booklet and these notes; side 2 carries the inside.

What is on the sheet

Two pieces, 100 × 70 mm, side by side near the top. Each piece is one sheet of the booklet: fold it in half on its ticks and it is 50 × 70 mm, the size of the board. Eight pages in total.

Check it before you cut

Hold the printed sheet up to a light. Page 2 must sit directly behind page 1, the right way up. If page 5 sits there instead, or a page is upside down, the printer flipped the second side on the short edge: print again with long-edge, or book, binding.

Assembly

  1. Cut along the grey lines. A steel rule and a knife give a straighter edge than scissors. Nothing outside the lines is kept.
  2. Fold each piece in half along its ticks with the outside faces showing: on the piece with page 1, page 1 stays out. Running a blunt rule along the fold first makes it crisper.
  3. Nest the pieces: the one with page 1 outermost, then the one with page 3 on its outer face. The tags name each piece's letter and the two pages on that face.
  4. Staple twice on the fold, about 10 mm in from each end, through both layers. Open the booklet and flatten the staple legs with a fingernail or the flat of the rule.

Page numbers sit at the foot of each page, odd on the right. A booklet that reads 1 to 8 in order, with no page upside down, is bound correctly.

Train mode

Nothing is at stake. A right answer lights CORRECT and the hand carries on.

A wrong one lights WRONG, and the book's play shows for a moment: HIT, STND, DOUB or SPLT.

Each hand ends with your accuracy blinking in place of the bankroll. 87.50 is 87.5 per cent.

Hold BANK to see it. It starts at zero each session.

Doubles and splits are always offered here.

Blackjack

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

  • Pictures count ten, an ace one or eleven.
  • Two cards making 21 is blackjack and beats any other 21.
  • The dealer stands on 17, soft or hard.
  • Double any first two cards, split or not.
  • A pair splits into up to four hands; split aces take one card.
  • No surrender. Equal totals push.

The end of a run

A win pays the bet, a push returns it, a loss takes it. A natural pays three to two.

Keep holding BANK and it flashes HIGH over the best bank you have had.

With less than $10 left, the whole balance goes on the next hand.

An empty bank lights LOSE and ends the run. Press any button to start again from $100.

Reach the top of the display and every light starts flashing. That is the jackpot.
